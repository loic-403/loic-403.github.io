Project 1 — Statistical arbitrage
Take a set of related assets.
Ask:

Can I identify statistically meaningful relationships between their returns?
Then investigate:
Correlation
Cointegration
Stationarity
Mean reversion
Hypothesis testing
Out-of-sample performance
Transaction costs
Multiple testing
Sharpe ratio
Drawdowns
The important part is not whether you discover a profitable strategy.
The important part is demonstrating that you understand how to avoid fooling yourself.
