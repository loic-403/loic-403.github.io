Consider a person betting on coin tosses. The coin is fair such that the probability of obtaining a head or a tail is $p=q=1/2$, respectively. If the person wins the bet, they receive twice the amount they put down. Otherwise, they lose their stake. At $t=0$, the gambler bets $1 on head. There is $p=1/2$ chance to win $2, whereas there is $q=1/2$ to lose $1. The expected value in this case is simply $$E = 2(1/2) + (-1)(1/2) = 1/2$$
